# SmasherGS

SmasherGS is a **LattePanda Mu** based Cyberdeck intended to serve as a rocketry ground station. 

<img width="1720" height="1801" alt="cyberdeck-main" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/59f899a3-c076-424b-a298-f15f6988b5ed" />

## Rocketry-Centered Features such as:
- GPS
- LORAWAN Connectivity
- Wifi and Bluetooth
- Long Battery Life
- Reverse Accessory Charging

## Durable Build

The structure is comprised of 2 aluminum plates sandwiching a 3D printed structure for high rigidity, drop resistance, and overall durability. 

It even features a full mechanical keyboard, despite its small size!
